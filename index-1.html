Sample question · Cardiology

A 58-year-old man has a collapsing pulse and a wide pulse pressure. Which valve lesion is most likely?

Aortic regurgitation. Rapid diastolic run-off into the left ventricle produces the water-hammer pulse and wide pulse pressure. In mitral stenosis the pulse is small volume and the murmur is a mid-diastolic rumble.

Why is troponin the preferred marker over CK-MB?
Troponin is more specific to cardiac muscle and stays raised for 7–14 days, which helps with late presentations. CK-MB returns to normal within 48–72 hours, so it is still useful for detecting re-infarction.
